My Story

I came to quantitative finance through computational physics. During my PhD at UNC–Chapel Hill, I developed computational methods for quantum many-body systems. I enjoyed turning a difficult question into a model and working through the calculation to reach a result I could explain.

Today, as a Lead Quantitative Analytics Specialist at Wells Fargo, I bring that approach to AI solutions and advanced analytics in the Model and AI Solutions (MAIS) team. I work with business units to identify useful applications of AI and lead working groups through development, deployment, and monitoring in a regulated financial environment.

Professional Journey

Current Role

As a Lead Quantitative Analytics Specialist, VP, I lead agentic AI projects and analytical work that helps teams understand business issues and make decisions. I also organize internal AI training and workshops, support AI adoption across business units, and participate in recruitment and entry-level training.

My earlier work covered Python risk analytics frameworks, equity P&L analysis, dashboards, and C++ derivatives pricing. Alongside that work, I mentored junior quants and served as a summer intern manager in 2024. In my Senior role, I focused on hands-on AI and analytics delivery. My Lead role adds broader project direction and coordination across business units.
