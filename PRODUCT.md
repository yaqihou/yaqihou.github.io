# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors seeking a quick introduction to Yaqi Hou, with optional deeper reading.
The user confirmed a balanced introduction for professional and research visitors.

## Product Purpose

A personal website presenting Yaqi Hou's professional work, computational physics
research, publications, education, and interests. The homepage must make the most
important information readily accessible without requiring a complete reading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript served by GitHub Pages from master at the domain
root. No static site generator, build dependency, or runtime template system.
Preserve all existing substantive content, public routes, citations and CVs.
Keep light mode as the default and provide an accessible dark-mode switch with a
smooth transition. Preserve the visitor's explicit preference across pages.

## Brand Commitments

Yaqi Hou. Tasteful personal presentation, not a dashboard. No homepage metric
tiles. The user explicitly delegated design, styling, animation, and composition, then
confirmed the spacious plum/lilac direction with expressive typography and subtle
interference animation.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing index, aboutme, professional, research, publications, 404, and historical
post HTML; the Atom feed; published academic and industry CVs; existing research
images and institution logos. These are the factual source, not a license to
invent claims. Untracked notes, JavaScript and the Quant CV predate this work.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors understand the person and their work quickly.
- Retain depth without forcing everyone through it.
- Preserve factual content while improving its presentation.
- Make navigation, reading, and theme selection accessible.
